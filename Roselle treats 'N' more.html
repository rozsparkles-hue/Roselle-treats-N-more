About Roselle treats 'N' more

Our Story

Roselle Treats 'N' More was founded in 2022 by Rosemary Udoffia Nwajei, building on years of passion and expertise in baking and confectionery. The business represents a dream brought to reality through dedication, skill, and an unwavering commitment to quality.

Our journey is rooted in perfecting traditional recipes and creating innovative treats that bring joy to our customers. With a foundation built on premium ingredients and meticulous craftsmanship, we deliver excellence in every bite.

We offer flexible delivery options - whether you prefer home delivery or convenient pick-up, we ensure your treats arrive fresh and perfect. Join us in celebrating the art of fine baking!
